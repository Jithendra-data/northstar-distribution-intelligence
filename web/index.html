PUBLIC PROJECT OVERVIEW

A complete analytics engineering project, from synthetic ERP data to executive decisions.

This project demonstrates how a distribution business can turn operational records into trusted KPIs, risk signals, and self-service analysis. The data is fictional, but the architecture mirrors a real BI delivery workflow: generate source records, clean and model them, check quality, export dashboard-ready JSON, and publish a live executive experience.

AutomatedGitHub Actions refreshes the synthetic scenario and redeploys the dashboard.
ModeledRaw records are shaped into dimensions, facts, KPIs, and analytics marts.
ControlledQuality controls reconcile duplicates, orphan records, invalid dates, and inventory exceptions.
